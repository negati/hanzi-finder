# Data Sources and Attribution

Hanzi Finder uses data derived from publicly available Chinese language resources.

## CC-CEDICT

Chinese–English dictionary data, including Pinyin readings and English definitions.

- Website: https://cc-cedict.org/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)

## Make Me a Hanzi

Character decomposition and related character information.

- Repository: https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi
- The `dictionary.txt` dataset is distributed under LGPL v3 or later.

## General Standard Chinese Characters

The application's 3,500-character selection is based on the Level 1 list of the _Table of General Standard Chinese Characters_ (通用规范汉字表).

- Source: https://github.com/ben-hua/general_standard_chinese

## Generated Dictionary

The `data/characters.json` file is generated from these resources using `build.py`.
